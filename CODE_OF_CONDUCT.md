# Code of Conduct

We aim to keep CorelMate technical, welcoming, and useful. Participants should be respectful, assume good faith, discuss ideas rather than people, and provide actionable feedback.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized behavior, and publishing private information without consent.

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that violate this standard. Report conduct concerns privately rather than escalating them in public threads.
